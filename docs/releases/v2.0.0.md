# Owlnest v2.0.0

**Draw your home right in the card.** No model to make anywhere else: draw your rooms, add floors and stairs, furnish, pick a style, and your lights and sensors find their place. These notes cover the changes since **v1.5.4**.

## New: draw your home

- **A plan editor in the card.** Draw a room with one drag, add walls, doors, windows, sliding doors and openings, and see the house built in 3D next to the plan as you go. Every wall has its own height and thickness; doors and windows have styles and colours, rooms have floors (wood, tiles, concrete, carpet).
- **Start from a template** (studio, one or two bedrooms, house) that comes furnished, or **trace over a picture** of your plan: load the estate agent's plan or a photo of a sketch, set its scale with one known length, and draw on top.
- **Invisible room boundaries.** An open kitchen and a living room can be two rooms without a wall between them.
- **A free furniture catalogue** of more than 200 pieces (Kenney and Quaternius packs, CC0), with top views on the plan. Furniture snaps to walls and corners, and small objects sit on tables and shelves by themselves. You can also add your own models (GLB or OBJ).
- **Lamps linked to Home Assistant.** A lamp from the catalogue can be linked to a light: its shade lights up with it, and it becomes a device on the card.
- **Import a Sweet Home 3D file (.sh3d).** It becomes an editable plan: walls, rooms, doors, windows, furniture and every level. Your devices, views and openings are carried over.

## New: floors and stairs

- **Several floors**, stacked in 3D. The floor below shows as a ghost on the plan so you draw right above it. A switch in the rooms bar moves between floors; the floors above the one you look at are hidden.
- **Stairs**: straight, L-shaped, U-shaped or spiral. You set the width, the turning side and the slope; the number of steps follows from the height. The floor above gets an opening with a railing.
- **Balconies and terraces**: a room with low walls stays open to the sky, and a flat roof gets its parapet.

## New: the Look tab

- **Ready-made styles** for the house: light render, brick, timber, stone, modern, dark. One tap sets the walls, the roof and the garden; the details stay one tap away.
- **Facades** in render, brick, timber or stone, an optional plinth, and a **pitched roof** in tiles, slate or zinc.
- **A garden** of low-poly trees and bushes, a grass grain on the lawn, ground styles and colours.
- **A sky that follows the weather**: clear, a few clouds or overcast from your weather entity, from day to dusk to a starry night. The clouds have the same faceted look as the house.
- At night, **the windows of lit rooms glow** from outside.

## New on the card

- **Rooms bar**: fly to a room in one tap, or double tap it in the 3D view. Saved views sit in the same bar.
- **Room panel**: the devices of a room with their switches, its temperature, and a button to turn the whole room off.
- **Wall views**, like in the Sims: walls up (with the roof), cutaway, ghost walls, walls down.
- **Device colours**: each kind of device has its colour, on the dots and in the lists; sensor values follow Home Assistant's precision and your language's decimal separator.
- **Tilt-and-turn windows** (thanks to Gogac for the request, #10): the same sash either swings open or tilts from the bottom, from a list of states or directly from an angle sensor such as the Shelly BLU Door/Window.
- **Guided tours** of the card, the editor and the plan, and a first steps guide.
- **Themes and layout**: choose what the card shows and where.

## Editor

- **Rebuilt editor**: tabs on top (Plan, Devices, Openings, Rules, Camera, Look, Settings), a docked panel, a phone layout.
- **One place per subject**: the plan is for the shape of the home, Look for everything you see, Settings for the technical side (scene, camera and dots, performance, language).
- **Devices grouped by room**, with live values, and **automatic placement**: the devices of each Home Assistant area go into the plan room of the same name.
- **Openings form** with a sensor picker and live state; while you pick a piece of the model, the one under the mouse lights up.
- **Camera tab** for saved views, **rules** written as sentences.
- The interface speaks French with "tu", and English.

## Performance

- Built for wall tablets: lamps can be toggled without freezing the card, dots move without reflowing the page, blur effects are kept for the high quality level.
- Changing the plan or the floor no longer recompiles the 3D shaders: dragging a wall stays smooth, and switching floors is immediate once a floor has been seen.
- An **on-device performance counter** (Settings, Performance, "Show performance") to check how a tablet keeps up.

## Fixed

- Ground placed under the lowest point of the house: a house shown on an upper floor no longer floats.
- Clear weather after rain restores the light.
- Intensity sliders work without a sun entity.

## Updating

Update **Owlnest** in HACS and **restart Home Assistant**, then force-reload the dashboard browser with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (**Cmd+Shift+R** on macOS). On a wall tablet, reload the dashboard page too.

Your scenes, devices, openings, rules and views are kept. If your scene used the previous sky, it now uses the new automatic sky; choose **Simple** in Look, Sky and light, to get the old one back.

For a manual installation, replace `config/custom_components/owlnest/` with the folder from this release, including `frontend/` and `brand/`, then restart Home Assistant.
