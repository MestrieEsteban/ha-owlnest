# Owlnest v1.5.0

Bring your own home in one gesture: **drop your Sweet Home 3D export on the card**. These notes cover the changes since **v1.4.0**.

## New: import your floor plan

- **Drag and drop.** Drop the folder exported by Sweet Home 3D onto the card, or its files, a zip of it, or a `.glb`. The card converts the model in your browser, textures included, sends it to Home Assistant and displays it. No Blender, no converter, no file to copy into `config/www`.
- **Or click "Import my floor plan"**, in the demo banner or at the top of the Scene section of the settings, and pick the files.
- A progress panel shows each step: reading, converting, sending, loading. If something is missing (no `.obj`, a `.gltf` instead of a `.glb`, a model over 300 MB), it says so in plain words.
- Imported models are stored in `config/owlnest/models/` and served by the integration. Only administrators can import.
- **Re-exporting your house keeps your work**: anchors and openings stay in place. Importing over the demo house starts from a clean scene.
- An imported plan takes precedence over the `model_url` of the card YAML, so dropping your plan always shows it.

To export from Sweet Home 3D: **3D view → Export to OBJ format**, export all the items into an empty folder, then drop that folder on the card.

## Improved

- **The camera frames the whole model** on first display and after an import, whatever its scale (metres or centimetres). Camera distance limits tuned for a previous model are reset on import.
- **One-click install buttons** in the README: open Owlnest in HACS, then add the integration, straight from GitHub.

## Fixed

- **When the integration does not respond**, the card no longer shows the demo house as if your scene were gone. It says the integration is not responding, that your scenes are safe, and never saves over them. It retries on its own, so your scene comes back after a restart without reloading the page.
- **A reload button** (↻) on the open scene, in the Scene section, reads it again from Home Assistant.
- **The scene chosen from the settings now applies to that card only.** A new card without `scene_id` used to open the scene picked on another card; it now shows the demo house. If one of your cards has no `scene_id` and you had picked its scene from the settings, pick it again once, or add `scene_id` to its YAML. Your scenes are not affected.
- Demo house: the kitchen table no longer sinks into the floor, the fridge stands against the wall, and the demo lamps light their room instead of glowing inside their shades.

## Updating

Update **Owlnest** in HACS and **restart Home Assistant**: the import needs the new integration, a reload is not enough. Then force-reload the dashboard browser with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (**Cmd+Shift+R** on macOS).

For a manual installation, replace `config/custom_components/owlnest/` with the folder from this release, including `frontend/` and `brand/`, then restart Home Assistant.
