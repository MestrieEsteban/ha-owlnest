# Owlnest v1.5.1

Your Sweet Home 3D doors and windows now come in ready to move. These notes cover the changes since **v1.5.0**.

## New: openings found on import

- **Doors and windows are recognised** in a Sweet Home 3D export. Right after the import, the card offers to add them: one click, and they are set up with the hinges on the right side and opening towards the inside of the home. You only pick the sensor of each one in the Openings tab.
- **Sliding doors and windows** are recognised too, including French windows with two panels on a shared rail: each panel slides towards the other.
- **Doors in angled walls** turn on their real hinges.
- **Cupboard, kitchen and appliance doors are left out**: they have hinges too, but nobody wants ten kitchen doors in their openings.
- Two-panel windows are named "Window 1 · leaf 1", "Window 1 · leaf 2", so both panels are easy to link to the same sensor.
- **Already have openings?** The card asks instead of guessing: keep yours, add only the new ones, or replace them. Replacing keeps the sensor, name, colours and any direction you fixed by hand for a door or window that is still there. The same choice is in the Openings tab.

Tested on seven real Sweet Home 3D plans: every opening turns on its hinges or stays in its rail.

## New: lighten a heavy plan

When a plan is heavy for a wall tablet, the import offers to lighten it, and explains what it changes:

- **Very detailed small objects** (dishes, plants, taps) are simplified. On a furnished flat: 902 000 → 390 000 triangles, 25.6 MB → 14.9 MB.
- **Textures larger than 1024 px** are reduced.
- **Walls, floors, doors and windows are never touched**, so openings and anchors stay exact.

It is an option, unchecked by default, and only offered when it helps. The card remembers your last choice.

## Updating

Update **Owlnest** in HACS, then force-reload the dashboard browser with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (**Cmd+Shift+R** on macOS). To get the openings, drop your plan again: they are recognised during the conversion.

For a manual installation, replace `config/custom_components/owlnest/` with the folder from this release, including `frontend/` and `brand/`, then restart Home Assistant.
