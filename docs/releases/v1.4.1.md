# Owlnest v1.4.1

This release fixes the demo house shipped with the integration. These notes cover the changes since **v1.4.0**.

## Fixed

- **The kitchen table sank into the floor**, so the kitchen lamp stood a few centimetres above the ground. The table overlapped the counter as well: it is gone, and the lamp now stands on the kitchen counter.
- **The fridge stands against the wall**, next to the stove, instead of in the middle of the kitchen.
- **The demo lamps light their room.** Their anchors sat inside the lamp shades, so the light glowed inside the object. They now sit just above the shades.
- The kitchen lamp anchor uses a lamp icon instead of a ceiling light.

If you already linked the demo lamps to your entities, your saved positions are kept. To get the new ones, move the anchors in the editor, or remove them from the scene: the card places the demo anchors again on an empty scene.

## Try it online

Owlnest now has a **[live demo](https://mestrieesteban.github.io/ha-owlnest/)**: the card running in your browser on a simulated home, with a guided tour of the lights, sensors, openings, rules and editor.

## Updating

Update **Owlnest** in HACS, restart Home Assistant, then force-reload the dashboard browser with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (**Cmd+Shift+R** on macOS).

For a manual installation, replace `config/custom_components/owlnest/` with the folder from this release, including `frontend/` and `brand/`, then restart Home Assistant.
